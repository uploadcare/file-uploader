v2 spike — upload tray

Compact corner panel showing per-file upload progress for an attached <uc-uploader>. The tray installs a router hook so the upload-list modal does NOT open after a file is added — the tray takes over as the visible upload-status surface. The plus button in the tray header re-opens the source picker.

Attach mode — mirrors <uc-uploader-regular>

Click "Upload files", pick something. The picker modal closes after add, and the tray appears in the bottom-right.
